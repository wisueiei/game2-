Use case: precise-object-edit / character redesign.
Image1 is EDIT TARGET: the six-cell UNDERFALL special-forms pixel roster. Image2 and Image3 are the user's NEW ASRIEL BODY / COSTUME REFERENCES, not edit targets.

NEW ASRIEL SILHOUETTE:
A youthful elegant WHITE GOAT-MONSTER boy, petite/slender in build but vertically elongated / tall-looking, as in the supplied references. Not a squat bulky furry adult, not a massive wide-chested king or broad triangular robe. Small head, slim shoulders/waist, long torso and clearly slender legs; about 4.5 to 5 heads tall. Body excluding cape roughly one-quarter of standing height wide. Keep his FULL BODY standing visible within this cell with padding, horn tips and bare feet unclipped.
Cream-white fluffy hair tuft/bangs, LONG floppy goat ears, small graceful curved ivory horns, short goat muzzle and dark small nose, calm slightly wistful determined expression. Clearly a goat monster, not a human face. Follow the delicate youthful proportions of Image3 while bringing the collar/chest details from Image2.

COSTUME faithful to references:
Dark royal PURPLE / INDIGO tailored tunic and long front tabard, narrow ivory/cream edging, a small clean pale Delta-Rune-like wing/triangle emblem on chest. Raised pointed dark collar behind the neck, light collar around neckline, TINY gold clasp/details. WHITE long sleeves / arms with defined small hands, not bulky armor. A small blue-lavender ribbon/sash tied at one side of waist as in Image3. Long black/dark-purple CAPE with lighter cream lining and purple trim flowing gently off to ONE side, but do not let cape balloon into a giant wide pyramid. Split lower tunic/robe around knee so slender white lower legs and small bare goat feet remain visible. No giant gold armor, trident, huge shoulder plates, boots, trousers obscuring feet or chibi giant head.
Standing front/slight three-quarter pose, one hand gently raised with just a tiny white/gold four-point star; other hand relaxed. No seated pose, ground scenery, rainbow lights, flooding green stars or large magic effects dominating body.

ART STYLE MUST STAY STRICT 8-BIT PIXEL ART:
Hard square pixel clusters, stepped silhouette, thin dark outline, LIMITED flat color ramps with just 2-3 tones per material. Sprite should look designed around a 48-to-64-pixel-tall grid then enlarged with nearest-neighbor, matching the existing sprite sheet rather than copying the references' smooth painted/anime style. Restrained but clear purple/white/cream/blue colors, maintain current palette liveliness (do NOT apply another global saturation bump). No gradients, glossy digital painting, blur, realistic cloth microtexture, soft lighting or 3D.
Do not copy flowers, paper background, artist credit, username, watermarks, video UI or celestial background from the reference illustrations. Only the Asriel DESIGN is referenced. Keep every text label and six-cell composition, and ensure full figure fits entirely in its own cell. No new characters or story changes.
