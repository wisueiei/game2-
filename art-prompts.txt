Built-in Imagegen — forest-clearing.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background for a playable top-down 2D pixel RPG, square 1024x1024. Create exquisitely crafted genuine pixel art of a small ROUND forest clearing seen in a classic overhead 3/4 RPG camera. A continuous dense ring of ancient broadleaf trees frames a roomy simple clearing. Tree trunks, roots, fern fronds, leaf clusters have beautiful hand placed pixel contours, stepped organic silhouettes, rich coherent 24 color palette and restrained clustered highlights, like a lovingly drawn 32-bit indie RPG. Center is walkable softly textured forest grass with subtle soil under moss, scattered tiny grouped flowers, no stone paving. Center 70 percent should remain open walkable grass, trees concentrated around perimeter. No central tree or foreground trunk across the clearing. Dark emerald and blue teal foliage, cool navy shadows, pale blue and ivory flower patches. Render diffuse moderately bright readable neutral moonlit source art: the game engine will dynamically darken it, do not bake spotlight or vignette. Flowers are tiny groundcover, not glowing light bulbs. Carefully organized detail, NO random noisy square mosaic, NO blocky rectangular placeholder trees, no blurry brushwork, no 3D, no photorealism. No characters, animals, monsters, water, ruins, architecture, doors, crystals, potions, text, lettering, UI or logos. Image fills square edge to edge, outer edges dark forest canopy, circular grassy clearing centered. This is final game art, not a mockup screenshot.

Built-in Imagegen — adventurer-sheet.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent pixel-art RPG sprite sheet. Create ONE sprite sheet with EXACTLY 4 columns by 4 rows of the SAME adorable tiny female adventurer, 16 sprites total, each exactly centered within equally sized square cells on a truly transparent background. No grid lines, no labels, no background, no shadow plates, no extra portraits. Short silver-cream hair, a modest brown pointed witch hat with muted gold ribbon, dark wine-red cloak over cream blouse, brown boots, cute expressive teal eyes, 3-head-tall chibi proportions. Fine intentional pixel art like detailed indie RPG sprites, organic silhouette with crisp one-pixel steps, coherent shading, not stacks of rectangles. Each cell has generous equal clear padding. Row 1: facing viewer, walk frames left foot forward, neutral, right foot forward, neutral. Row 2: facing screen-left, four walk frames. Row 3: facing away, four walk frames. Row 4: story poses of same character facing viewer: falling startled with arms out and fluttering cloak; lying on her side after landing; seated on ground propped up by hands; halfway rising on one knee. Consistent size, hat, hair, clothes, light and scale across all 16 cells. Every hat and foot completely inside its cell. Transparent alpha outside character. Canvas 1024x1024, cells 256x256 with sprites about 145px wide and 200px tall. Pixel clusters, not antialiased digital painting.
